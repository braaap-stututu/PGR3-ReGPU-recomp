Game

Game

Work in progress: boot, menus, car purchase and races run, with sound; the London race matches the console. The race picture is drawn at your resolution's scale but centred, not yet stretched to the window. Saves and settings live in the user folder (Settings tab). Controls: an Xbox controller, or the keyboard as player 1.

Benchmark

The game's own race AI drives your car through the London race (6 cars, 3 laps) while the frame rate is measured, then the game closes by itself. It races on the test route's own profile (out\user-route), so your save doesn't change, with the settings on these tabs: for the full speed set Max FPS 360 and VSync off. The AI drives a little differently each time: run it a few times before comparing.
